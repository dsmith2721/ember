Ember(beta)

Keep your faith fire lit

Ember is a free app for daily Bible reading, prayer, devotionals and growing in faith. It works right in your browser, and you can put it on your home screen like any other app.

Open Ember

Put Ember on your home screen

There's nothing to download from an app store. Pick your device and follow the steps.

  1. Open in Safari

    Open this page in Safari, then tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen: a square with an arrow pointing up.

    Don't see it? Tap the ••• button first, then Share.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.

  3. Tap Add

    Leave the name as Ember and tap Add in the top-right corner.

  4. You're all set

    Ember is on your home screen. Tap the flame to open it, just like an app.

  1. Open in Chrome

    Open this page in Chrome, then tap the three dots in the top-right corner.

  2. Install the app

    Tap Add to home screen. On some phones it says Install app.

  3. Tap Install

    Tap Install (or Add) to confirm.

  4. You're all set

    Ember is on your home screen. Tap the flame to open it.

  1. Click the install icon

    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar: a small screen with a down arrow.

    On a Mac with Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock.

  2. Click Install

    Click Install. Ember opens in its own window and gets a shortcut on your computer.

What's inside

  • A flame that grows as you read, pray and serve each day
  • Streaks, daily goals and an Ember jar that fills as you grow
  • The Bible in dozens of versions, plus free commentaries
  • Devotionals, classic Christian books, podcasts and Christian radio
  • Scripture memory practice and guides for sharing your faith
